Why Cocometric?

In the coconut industry, evaluating the thickness of the coconut meat is crucial for quality grading and pricing. However, the traditional method, which relies on expertise, can sometimes lack precision. Our application introduces a modern, non-destructive approach using advanced image processing technology. By analyzing photos of coconuts, we can quickly and accurately calculate the thickness of the coconut meat — helping to reduce costs, increase efficiency, and add value to your products.
